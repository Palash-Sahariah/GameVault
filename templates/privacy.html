Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to GameVault. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use the GameVault website and its browser-based games.

This locally stored information generally remains on your device and is not the same as information stored on a GameVault user account.

When advertising services are enabled, third parties may collect or use information according to their own privacy policies and applicable advertising technologies.

Users should review the privacy policies of the relevant advertising providers for more information about their practices.

5. Game and Website Data

GameVault games may use browser storage to provide features such as game progress, scores, fictional gameplay balances, shopping carts and collections.

Clearing your browser's site data or local storage may remove this information.

6. Third-Party Services

GameVault may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, security or other website functionality.

These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.
